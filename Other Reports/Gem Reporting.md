## Critical Structural & Textual Defects

Before addressing line-level corrections, five major structural corruptions require immediate editorial intervention:

* **Title Error (p. 1):** The book title is printed as **`॥ धर्माकूतम् ॥`** instead of **`॥ धर्मकूतम् ॥`**.


* **Meaning-Reversing Omission (p. 14):** In the prose introducing *Jyotirnibandha*, **`अत एव विचारेण प्रस्थितस्य फलावाप्तिर्घुणाक्षरतुल्येत्युक्तम्`** drops the negative prefix `अ-`. The quoted verse immediately below (`अतर्किते...`) proves it must read **`अत एवाविचारेण प्रस्थितस्य`** ("for one who sets out *without* deliberation, success is accidental like worm-eaten letters").


* **Duplicated Text Block (p. 15):** The heading and entire *Yoginī* verse (`अथ शूलनक्षत्राणि । शतमखेन्दु... योगिनी ॥`) are pasted **twice consecutively**—the first copy bolded with the typo `थूलनक्षत्राणि`, the second unbolded with `शूलनक्षत्राणि`.


* **Massive Lacuna & Mid-Word Truncation (p. 31):** Immediately after `अत्र सेनाहर्षादिकं आगामिजयज्ञापकमिति सूचितम् ॥` (Yuddha Kāṇḍa, Sarga 4), the text abruptly jumps several sargas forward to the Vibhīṣaṇa episode, starting mid-word at **`षेण व्यथते वज्रं वज्रेण भिद्यते`** (missing **`विषं वि`** before `षेण`, plus the entire intervening commentary between Sarga 4 and the Vibhīṣaṇa-śaraṇāgati section).


* **Abrupt End-of-File Truncation (p. 32):** The document cuts off mid-sentence immediately after the citation header **`तथा च राजधर्मे-`**, omitting the quoted verse.

## Systemic DTP & Formatting Mistakes

1. **Orphaned Punctuation (`।`, `॥`, `इति ।`):**
* Daṇḍas and closing markers frequently wrap alone onto the next line due to hard spaces or tight right margins: **p. 2** (`।` at start of line, and standalone `।`), **p. 3** (`।`, `इति ।`), **p. 4** (`।`, `इति ।`), **p. 5** (`।`, `।`, `॥ इति ।`), **p. 6** (`इति ।` ×2), **p. 7** (`फलम् ।`, `॥ इति ।`, `इति ।`), **p. 10** (`।`), **p. 11** (`।`), **p. 13** (`च ।`), **p. 14** (`।`), **p. 17** (`॥`), **p. 18** (`। इति`), **p. 19** (`।`, `इति ।`), **p. 21** (`॥`, `इति ।`), **p. 22** (`।`), **p. 25** (`॥` ×2), **p. 27** (`इति ।` ×2), **p. 29** (`इति ।`), **p. 30** (`इति ।` ×2), **p. 32** (standalone `।` at very top of page).




2. **Prose & Verse Indentation Collisions:**
* Prose commentary inherits verse indentation directly beneath quotes with zero spacing on **p. 3** (`अनेन प्राकृत एव...`), **p. 8** (`अत एव धृतराष्ट्रः...`), **p. 12** (`अनेन राज्ञा...`), **p. 22** (`अनेन मध्यमगुल्मे...`), and **p. 31** (`अनेन सेनाहर्षादिकं...`).


* Conversely, verses lose indentation and run like left-aligned prose on **p. 6** (`प्रियेण दानेन...`), **p. 7** (`निमित्तराशिरेकतो...`), **p. 8** (`गावल्गणे...`), **p. 16** (`वारशूलोऽपि...`), **p. 17** (`लग्नबलं रत्नमालायां - करोति...`), **p. 18** (`सेनापतिं... । इति फलमूलवता...` — two separate citations fused together), **p. 19** (`मधुमता चाशु...`), **p. 21** (`दुर्बलाः क्षतसाराश्च...`), **p. 22** (`संहतान् योधयेत्...`), **p. 24** (`माङ्गल्यशब्दान्...`), **p. 27** (`स्वं स्वं वर्गं...`), and **p. 29** (`अहुताशेऽनलरूपं...`).




3. **Inconsistent Bolding & Styling of Citation Headers:**
* The exact same source names alternate arbitrarily between bold and regular weight (e.g., `राजधर्मे-` is regular on p. 2, 3, 12, 19, 21, but **bold** on p. 6, 10, 19, 24, 31, 32; `कामन्दके-` is regular on p. 4, 8, 20, but **bold** on p. 5, 6, 11, 19; `रत्नमालायाम्-` is **bold** on p. 16 top & p. 24, but regular on p. 16 bottom & p. 17).


* Some lines have only a bold thick hyphen attached to regular text (e.g., `द्रोणपर्व...वचनम्-` p. 3, `प्रतिपादितमुद्योगे-` p. 8, `राजधर्मे-` p. 12, `मनुः-` p. 22).




4. **ASCII / Non-Devanagari Punctuation Contamination:**
* ASCII vertical pipe `|` is used instead of Devanagari daṇḍa `।` on **p. 5** (`सूचितम् | तव`), **p. 17** (`वा स्तः | स्त्र्याय`), and **p. 18** (`सूचितम् | तथाच`).


* Modern commas `,` and colons `:` intrude inconsistently into Sanskrit prose (**p. 2** `कृतं, ... नीतः,`, `ततः,`, **p. 8** `तेषां,`, **p. 15** list commas, **p. 16** `वारशूलोऽपि,`, `नक्षत्रयोगिन्यपि :`, **p. 17** fn 1 `त्रिगाः,`, **p. 22** `वानरयूथपेषु,`).


* Citation introducers mix five different styles: `-`, ` -`, `:-`, `—`, and `।` (e.g., `यात्रायां प्रशस्तनक्षत्राणि—` vs `मध्यमानि वर्ज्यानि च नक्षत्राणि ।` on p. 14; `ऋषि विषये` with no punctuation at all on p. 26).




5. **Unresolved Critical Apparatus Brackets Left Inline:**
